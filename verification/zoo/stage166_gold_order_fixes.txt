Stage 166 (2026-11-26, docs section 170): section 169's gold order-path gaps fixed, and one refinement to MNQ's 168.

MgcOvernightCarry.cs (as MnqCleanBand.cs in 168): Exit() records its bar; OnOrderUpdate marks a refused market exit
and tracks the CarryLong / CarryShort entry (Working / PartFilled / Filled / Cancelled / Rejected); at every bar, after
its bookkeeping, RetryExit() sends a refused exit again ("Exit retry"), and a part-filled entry's unfilled rest is
cancelled.

The refinement (both strategies): the unfilled rest is cancelled whether or not the filled part is still open. The
first gold run showed why: most gold trades are stopped on their entry bar (section 110), so the position was already
flat at the bar's close and the rest stayed working, blocking every later entry. MnqCleanBand.cs gets the same
condition (its test trade happened to be still open).
