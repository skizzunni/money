Stage 164 (2026-11-26, docs section 168): section 167's two order-path gaps fixed in MnqCleanBand.cs.

1. A refused exit is sent again. Exit() records its bar and the entry it closes; OnOrderUpdate marks a refused market
   exit (any order that is not an entry, the stop or the target) while a position is open; RetryExit(), at the start
   of every bar (any time of day, before the outside-hours and carried-position checks, without skipping the bar's
   bookkeeping), sends the exit again ("Exit retry") when the last exit was refused or did not close the position by
   the next bar. Nothing exits twice: the retry runs only while "exiting" and not flat.
2. A part-filled entry's unfilled rest is cancelled at the first bar with the position open (CancelOrder on the
   entry order tracked from OnOrderUpdate); the filled part trades on with its stop.

Tests (tests/test_ninjatrader_clean.py, the NinjaTrader stand-in):
- test_clean_band_rejected_exit_retries: a midday exit refused -> the trade is closed within one bar (by the retry, or
  first by its own resting stop, which never left); a 15:55 exit refused -> "Exit retry" at the next session's first
  bar on a regular-hours chart (the earliest possible; on the user's ETH chart the 16:00 bar), with the "was refused" and
  "sending it again" Output lines. Before: carried overnight and closed at 09:40 by its target.
- test_clean_band_partial_entry_rest_cancelled: an entry filling 1 of 3 and never the rest -> the rest is cancelled
  and every later trade matches the baseline (93 trades after it; before: no trade after it). CushionGuard 0 so the
  trades do not depend on the 1-contract loss's effect on the account.
No backtest trade changes (fills are whole and exits accepted in every backtest).
