Stage 46 (2026-11-24): half days (the CME index futures' 13:15 early close: the Friday after Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve)

The finding (research/stress/torture.py, "half days": sessions cut at 13:15 with a trade open): a band trade still open
at the cut was held into the next session (overnight 1, both runs). On a real half day the next bar is Sunday evening:
the trade would have carried over a weekend, protected only by NinjaTrader's session-close exit (which depends on the
trading-hours template listing the early close).

The fix, MnqCleanBand.cs and MgcOvernightCarry.cs (three layers):
  1. the session's actual end from the trading-hours template (SessionIterator.ActualSessionEnd, as MnqMomentumC.cs
     FlatBy): out 5 minutes before it when that is before the usual exit (13:15: out at the 13:10 open; COMEX's 13:45:
     at the 13:40 open); no new MNQ entry from then on;
  2. the calendar, whatever the template says: the Friday after Thanksgiving (Nov 23-29) and Dec 24: out by the 13:10
     open;
  3. the backstop: a trade still open outside regular hours, or when a new session starts, goes out on the next bar.
The Pine scripts have 2 and 3 (TradingView has no early-close calendar).

A bug the new tests caught before it shipped: the calendar's 13:10 was first written 1310 (it is minutes from midnight:
790), which left the exit at 15:55. Fixed; the calendar test fails without the fix.
