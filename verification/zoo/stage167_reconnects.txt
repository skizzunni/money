Stage 167 (2026-11-26, docs section 171): what the strategies do on a restart or reconnect with a trade open, by reading
MnqCleanBand.cs / MgcOvernightCarry.cs. Real NinjaTrader cannot be run here, so this rests on the code and on
NinjaTrader 8's documented behaviour, not on a live test.

- Both use StartBehavior.WaitUntilFlat: when enabled (or re-enabled after a restart) they replay the loaded history; if
  that replay ends inside a trade, they place no live orders until the replayed position is flat. They do not adopt
  the account's real position (no "AdoptAccountPosition"). On reaching realtime, MnqCleanBand re-sizes from the
  account's balance (State.Realtime handling).
- Neither sets ConnectionLossHandling, so NinjaTrader's default applies (in NinjaTrader 8: recalculate on reconnect).
  A short drop with the strategy still enabled normally leaves its orders and position tracking in place; a long one,
  a restart or a disable / enable recalculates from history as above.
- The risk: a real position open in the account that the strategy no longer manages. Its stop and target were sent
  to the broker and normally stay working there (they protect it), but the strategy's own exits (MNQ 15:55, gold 14:15,
  the band-back-inside exit, the retry of 168 / 170) are not sent for it, so it could be held past the session.
- No code change (the right fix depends on NinjaTrader behaviour that cannot be tested here; adopting account positions
  without a test would be riskier than the manual check). Added to "Running it" step 13: after any disconnect,
  restart or disable / enable while a trade is open, compare the account's position (Control Center > Positions) with
  the strategy's (Strategies tab); if the account holds a position the strategy does not show, close it by hand
  before the session ends, and check its stop is working.
