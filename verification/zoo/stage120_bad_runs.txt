Notes (2026-11-25, docs section 121): an expectation for live, not a rule.
- MNQ alone (no gold), the shipped rules, no cushion guard (bench.py and harness.account have none; live, the guard
  cuts to 1 contract, a smaller day stop and no gold within $1,500 of the floor, so live lows should be a little
  shallower and recoveries slower). The median cushion low ($1,175 / $1,245) means the guard would act at some point
  in most fresh years.
- A fall from the high can pass $2,000 without ending the account: the floor stops trailing at $50,000.
- The worst day can pass the $600 day stop: it is checked between trades, and a trade that starts at -$590 can lose
  its full stop.
