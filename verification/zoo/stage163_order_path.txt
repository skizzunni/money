stand-in (ninjatrader/tests/Sim; synthetic history, MaxContracts 3, StartProfit 5000).

Covered and safe (existing tests): a stop cancelled or rejected with the position open -> out at market (that bar's
close, or live 2 s after the cancel): test_clean_band_never_without_a_stop (cancel / reject x backtest / live); a
target's OCO cancel of the stop never exits twice: test_clean_band_oco_cancel_does_not_exit_twice.

Probed (no test existed for MnqCleanBand):
1. A rejected entry (SimRejectEntriesAt): the trade does not happen, later trades unaffected. Safe.
2. A rejected exit (RejectExitsAt on the 15:50 bar of a trade out at 15:55): the position was NOT closed that day; it
   was carried overnight and closed the next morning at 09:40 by its target. Cause: Exit() sets exiting = true and
   pos = 0; when the exit is rejected the position stays open, exiting never clears (it clears only when flat), and
   that flag silences every later guard: the carried-position exit on the next session's first bar (carried && mp != 0
   && !exiting), the outside-regular-hours exit and the no-stop watchdog. Live NinjaTrader's own exit on session close
   (IsExitOnSessionCloseStrategy, 60 s before the close) may still flatten it, but if that is refused too, nothing in
   the strategy tries again. A real gap: to fix (an exit not filled by the next bar is sent again, from the position).
3. A partially filled entry whose rest never fills (SimPartialEntryAt, first 1 of 3, the rest never): the 1 contract
   trades and is stopped out, but the rest of the entry order stays working and the strategy places no further trade
   for the rest of the run (1 trade after it against 4+ in the baseline). Market orders on MNQ practically always fill
   whole, so this is rare, but it would stop the bot silently. To fix: cancel an entry's unfilled rest at the next bar.
Both fixes change MnqCleanBand.cs only in order handling (no backtest trade changes), with tests for each.
